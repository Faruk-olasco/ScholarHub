ScholarHub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

ScholarHub is an Android app that aggregates publicly available scholarship, fellowship and grant announcements and links to the original publishers. It is developed and operated by Faruk Olasco ("we").

Information we collect

  • Alerts (optional): if you subscribe to alerts we store the email address or Telegram chat ID and keywords you provide, solely to send you matching opportunities. Contact us to unsubscribe or delete this data.
  • Saved items & preferences: stored only on your device; never sent to us.
  • Technical data: our database provider (Supabase) logs standard request metadata (IP address, timestamps) for security.

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, contacts, location or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and other data to show relevant ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Users in the EEA/UK are asked for consent via Google's consent form. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Third-party links

Listings link to external websites (universities, governments, publishers). Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The service is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Data retention and deletion

Alert subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. To request access to or deletion of your data, email us from the address you subscribed with; we will action the request within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always available at this address.
