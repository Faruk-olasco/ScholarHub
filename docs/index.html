What is ScholarHub?

ScholarHub is a free scholarship app for students in Nigeria, Africa and around the world. It automatically searches hundreds of university, government, embassy and foundation websites twice every day and brings you fully funded scholarships, fellowships, grants, exchange programmes and summer schools for undergraduate, master's, PhD and postdoctoral study. Every listing shows the application deadline, funding type, level of study and the official link to apply.

Beyond scholarships, the app keeps you informed with school news (JAMB, WAEC, NECO, NUC and Nigerian universities and polytechnics), daily tech updates, and entry-level jobs — graduate trainee programmes, internships, NYSC placements, NGO and remote roles.

Frequently asked questions

Is ScholarHub free?

Yes. The app is completely free to download and use and is supported by small ads.

Which scholarships does it cover?

Fully funded and partially funded scholarships for international students in the UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Japan, China, Australia, Turkey and many more, plus Nigerian scholarships such as PTDF, NNPC, Shell, MTN and state bursaries when they open.

What if a scholarship has no deadline shown?

It means the official page has not stated one. The listing is treated as open until the source removes it.

Does it work outside Nigeria?

Yes. Scholarships are worldwide and you can filter by region or country. School news and most jobs focus on Nigeria, with remote and international graduate programmes included.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet — ScholarHub is currently available for Android on Google Play.
